**JACK OF FACES** @JackOfFaces [2025-07-17](https://x.com/JackOfFaces/status/1945871879761256941)

The worst thing you have to fight in this life - you have to fight alone.

Be it betrayal, temptation, grief, or a secret emptiness in your heart.

In your most difficult task - no soul is able to help you.

 If more humans understood this, we would be far kinder to one another.
